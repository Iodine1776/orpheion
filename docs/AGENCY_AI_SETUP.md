# Orpheion: current brand and offer standard
Effective September 19, 2026. Supersedes prior marketing direction for new offers; existing agreed customer terms remain controlling.

Brand: Orpheion. Descriptor: Practical AI consulting for independent insurance agencies. Product: Agency AI Setup. AI Risk Desk may describe separately scoped future advisory work; it is not a promise of unlimited access or monitoring.

Positioning: Help agency teams put AI to work with sensible rules, two useful workflows and human review. Lead with useful adoption and explain safeguards concretely. Sell implementation of guidance and staff adoption, not a document collection or immunity from loss.

Standard price $1,500 total. Setup target ten business days after agreed start and complete permitted inputs; follow-up around day 30. One team up to 25, review up to ten tools/tasks, develop two workflows. 45-minute kickoff, 45-minute walkthrough, 30-minute follow-up or agreed written substitutes. One revision round per deliverable and one consolidated workflow adjustment at follow-up. Ongoing advisory separately scoped/priced; no automatic renewal. No included client reports. Existing accepted terms are not retroactively changed.

Keep stone/bronze colors, serif headings, lyre mark and small founder portrait. Describe Sam's verified insurance sales and commercial account management experience. No guru title, invented credentials, testimonials, results, scarcity or liability guarantees. Fictional sample materials must say so. Demonstrations are prepared illustrations unless actually run and documented.

Use clear questions and natural language. Primary CTA invites a conversation with Sam; secondary CTA shows work. Explain that general email is AI-connected and confidential intake requires a separate written release. Never use the residential address.
