# Client Expectations & Scope  
**Orpheion — Async Design → Production Subscription**

This document is the product. Paying for a subscription means you agree to these terms.

---

## 1. What you are buying

A **monthly, pause-anytime** production queue: locked design → production frontend (Next.js on Vercel), delivered **100% asynchronously** (shared board + Loom).

- **One active request at a time**
- Fixed monthly price (see your invoice / checkout tier)
- No sales calls, discovery workshops, or live meetings by default

**Typical tier:** $3,500–$4,500 / month  
Exact rate is confirmed at signup and may change for new clients when capacity is full.

**Optional add-on:** Kickoff Sprint (full multi-page stand-up) — quoted separately using the same rules below, then you continue on the monthly queue for ongoing work.

---

## 2. Who this is for

- Design agencies / brand studios with overflow production work  
- Product or marketing teams that already have UI in Figma (or equivalent)

**You should already have designs.** This is not a design, strategy, or UX subscription.

---

## 3. What you must provide per request

A request is not “active” until the packet is complete:

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| **Design source** | Locked Figma (or equivalent), inspectable; final layout, type, color, spacing |
| **Request brief** | Clear outcome (e.g. “Build Pricing section + wire form to HubSpot”) |
| **Assets** | Logos, fonts/licenses, icons, imagery needed for that request |
| **Copy** | Final or near-final text (or explicit OK to ship placeholders) |
| **Access** | Repo/Vercel/domain/DNS as needed, or written handoff path |
| **Integrations** | Form destination, analytics IDs, embeds — if in scope for that request |
| **Decision-maker** | One person who replies on the board within **one business day** |

Incomplete packets stay in the queue until fixed. That wait does not count as delivery delay on our side.

---

## 4. What “one active request” means

- **One request** = one coherent deliverable you could demo in a single Loom (e.g. one landing page, one major section set, one component system slice, a defined set of UI fixes).  
- Large builds are **split into sequenced requests** (Request A → done → Request B).  
- You may submit multiple items to the backlog; only the top one is active.  
- Changing the active request mid-flight restarts that request unless we agree the change is trivial.

**Rough size guide (standard subscription):**  
About **one marketing page or up to ~3 related sections** per request, or an equivalent UI fix/CRO variant bundle. Bigger = we split or quote a Kickoff Sprint / change order.

See also `ONE_REQUEST.md`.

---

## 5. What is included

- Production implementation from locked design  
- Next.js + Tailwind (Shadcn where it fits)  
- Motion only where specified in the design  
- Forms via standard hosted/SaaS paths (no custom app backend on the standard plan)  
- Responsive behavior aligned to provided breakpoints  
- Vercel preview + path to production/handoff as agreed  
- Short Loom + notes when a request is ready for review  

---

## 6. What is not included

- Zoom/Meet calls (paid exception only if explicitly offered)  
- UX strategy, IA, copywriting, art direction, or design exploration  
- “Make it better” beyond the provided file  
- Custom auth, dashboards, multi-role apps, complex CMS/backend, or open-ended product engineering  
- SEO retainers, content, photography  
- Same-day / rush guarantees unless purchased as a defined rush add-on  
- Unlimited device QA beyond modern evergreen browsers (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge — latest)

Out-of-scope work needs a **written change order** (price + timing) before it starts.

---

## 7. Turnaround & schedule

- Target: **active request updates within 24–48 hours** on business days, capacity permitting  
- Typical completion for an in-size request: **2–5 business days** after the packet is complete  
- Hours are **async pockets**, not a dedicated full-time seat on your Slack  
- Weekends/holidays are off unless agreed in writing  

If you need a full site stood up on a fixed deadline, use a **Kickoff Sprint**, not the subscription queue alone.

---

## 8. Design lock, reviews & revisions

1. The file attached to the request is **locked** when that request becomes active.  
2. **In-request fixes** cover mismatches to that locked design, bugs we introduced, or blockers we caused.  
3. **Design changes** after lock (new layouts, reflowing copy, extra sections, redesigns) = new request or change order.  
4. **Review window:** **two business days** after the delivery Loom to report in-scope fixes on the board. Silence = accepted.  
5. There is no unlimited revision bank. Iteration happens by **queueing the next request**.

---

## 9. Communication rules

- **Primary:** Shared task board + Loom  
- **Response time:** Both sides within **one business day** on blockers  
- **One decision-maker** on your side; conflicting stakeholder feedback pauses the request  
- Scope lives on the board — if it isn’t there, it isn’t in the queue  

---

## 10. Acceptance criteria (per request)

A request is done when:

- The agreed deliverable matches the locked design within normal web-vs-Figma production tolerance  
- Preview is shared and the review window has been used or has elapsed  
- In-scope fixes from that review are completed  
- Deploy/handoff steps agreed for that request are finished  
- Listed integrations for that request are wired or explicitly deferred in writing  

“Pixel-perfect” means **faithful production translation**, not infinite polish after lock.

---

## 11. Billing, pause & cancel

- Billed **monthly in advance** (auto-pay preferred)  
- **Pause:** Allowed between requests; pause stops new active work and billing per your plan’s pause rules (notice on the board before the next billing date)  
- **Cancel:** Anytime before renewal; access to the queue ends at period end  
- No refunds for unused days inside an active billing period once work has been available to you  
- Kickoff Sprint fees (if any) follow their invoice terms (often 50/50 or 100% upfront) and are separate from the subscription  

---

## 12. Access & ownership

- On payment for the period in which work was delivered: you own that delivered frontend code for your project (excluding third-party tools, fonts, and licenses you provide)  
- Orpheion may reuse non-confidential patterns and components  
- You own ongoing Vercel/domain/SaaS bills unless a written addendum says otherwise  

---

## 13. Capacity & fair use

- Subscription = **one active request**, not unlimited parallel workstreams or a full-time engineer  
- Sustained volume beyond one-at-a-time throughput may require: waiting in queue, a higher tier (if offered), or a second seat  
- Orpheion may pause intake of new subscribers when capacity is full  

---

## 14. How to start

1. Confirm tier and start date.  
2. Pay first month (and Kickoff Sprint invoice if applicable).  
3. Get board access + submit Kickoff packet / first request.  
4. First request becomes active when the packet is complete.
